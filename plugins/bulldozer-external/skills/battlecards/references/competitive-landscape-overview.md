The "if it comes up, say" column is the whole point. A rep who has one honest sentence for an unfamiliar name keeps control of the call; a rep who has nothing either improvises a claim that later turns out false, or goes quiet. One sentence beats a page nobody read.

**Give the status quo its own line.** The most frequent "competitor" in most B2B evaluations isn't a named vendor at all — it's the spreadsheet, the incumbent manual process, or doing nothing. `bdzCreateCompetitor` only tracks real companies (a name and a website, or an existing company record), so status quo never enters the tool the way a named competitor does — write it into the table by hand, or it silently disappears from the set reps are prepared for. The "if it comes up, say" sentence for this row answers "why change now," not "why us over them."
