# Category Concept Templates

Starting points for the "Category Templates" step of `ad-creative-brief`. Per category,
concepts each naming one of the 16 Universal Angles (Step 2 of the main skill), plus
Objection Handling (Step 2's 17th angle, real in Bulldozer's own creative library but not
in the table), and a visual direction. **Never ship a template unmodified** — every
concept must trace back to the product's real value proposition, proof points, and actual
audience. For B2B/SaaS specifically, search Bulldozer's creative library first (Step 2) —
it holds real, shipped examples of this exact category and will out-perform a template.

## DTC E-Commerce

| Concept | Angle | Visual direction |
|---|---|---|
| Unboxing Anticipation | Curiosity/Intrigue | Package on a doorstep, shot top-down |
| Price Anchor | Price/Value | Product beside a stack of priced alternatives |
| Ingredient/Feature Callout | Ingredient/Feature Spotlight | Split image: the one feature vs. a generic competitor's |
| Bundle Value | Price/Value | Flat lay of everything included |
| Founder Story | Emotional/Story | Authentic founder portrait, not a stock photo |

## B2B / SaaS

| Concept | Angle | Visual direction |
|---|---|---|
| Time Savings | Transformation/Result | Before/after of the same workflow |
| ROI / Metric Lift | Social Proof | Dashboard screenshot with the improved metric highlighted |
| Feature Announcement | Education/How-To | Product screenshot with the new feature circled |
| Competitive Migration | Comparison/Alternative | Side-by-side comparison table, named competitor |
| Case Study | Social Proof | Customer logo paired with their metric gain |
| Objection Rebuttal | Objection Handling | Direct quote of the objection, crossed out, with the answer beside it |

## Health & Wellness

| Concept | Angle | Visual direction |
|---|---|---|
| Practitioner Recommended | Authority/Expert | Clean, clinical styling — no lifestyle clutter |
| Natural Alternative | Comparison/Alternative | Product with botanical/ingredient elements |
| Daily Routine | Lifestyle/Aspirational | Morning-light lifestyle shot, product in hand |
| Results Over Time | Transformation/Result | Clean infographic, stated timeframe |
| Ingredient Education | Ingredient/Feature Spotlight | Macro shot of the ingredient next to the product |

## Beauty & Skincare

| Concept | Angle | Visual direction |
|---|---|---|
| Before/After | Transformation/Result | Clean split comparison, consistent lighting both sides |
| Texture Close-Up | Ingredient/Feature Spotlight | Macro shot of the product's texture or application |
| Routine Integration | Lifestyle/Aspirational | Bathroom-counter lifestyle shot, product mid-routine |
| Dermatologist Tested | Authority/Expert | Clinical, trustworthy styling |
| Ingredient Stack | Ingredient/Feature Spotlight | Two hero ingredients shown combining toward one result |

## Food & Beverage

| Concept | Angle | Visual direction |
|---|---|---|
| Taste Description | Emotional/Story | Appetizing pour, bite, or serving shot |
| Nutrition Angle | Education/How-To | Nutrition highlights overlaid on the product |
| Convenience | Price/Value | Quick-prep sequence, stated time |
| Origin Story | Emotional/Story | Source or farm imagery paired with the product |
