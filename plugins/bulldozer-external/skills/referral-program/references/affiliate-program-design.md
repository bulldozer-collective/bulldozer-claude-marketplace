# Affiliate program design — commission, recruitment, tools

For content creators, bloggers, and distribution partners. For the full recruitment tiers, deal-registration and dispute mechanics of a standalone program, use `affiliate-program` — this stays scoped to what changes when affiliates are layered onto an existing referral motion.

## Commission structure

No audited study sets a market-standard rate — every figure below comes from affiliate-software vendors and directories describing their own customers' programs, not from an independent survey, so treat it as a starting range to negotiate from, not a benchmark to defend.

- SaaS: recurring commission is now the dominant model (roughly two-thirds to three-quarters of programs by these vendor counts) over a flat one-time bounty, commonly 20–30% for the first 6–12 months, sometimes stepping down for renewals rather than running flat indefinitely
- E-commerce: 5–15% of sale value
- High-ACV: Flat fee per qualified introduction ($500–$2k) plus percentage of closed revenue
- Whatever the number, keep it below what the same customer costs to acquire through paid channels, and decide the cookie/attribution window (last-touch vendors converge on 30–90 days, no single standard) up front — it's the term most affiliate disputes actually turn on, and the launch checklist in the main skill doesn't call it out on its own.

## Recruitment

1. Start with people already writing about you (Google: "best [category]" and find who lists competitors)
2. Check who links to competitors (Ahrefs backlink analysis)
3. Reach out to newsletter and podcast hosts in your space
4. List your program on affiliate directories (CJ Affiliate, formerly Commission Junction; Awin, which fully absorbed ShareASale in 2025 — the standalone ShareASale platform closed October 6, 2025, and its advertisers and publishers now run on Awin's platform under one login. Treat "ShareASale" as a legacy brand you'd apply to through Awin, not a second, separate network alongside it)

Steps 2 and 3 target people already promoting a competitor — expect coexistence, not replacement, in the first few months: an affiliate rarely drops an existing partner for a new one until the new one is actually paying out. Decide up front whether you're fine being listed alongside that competitor in their content, or whether the relationship only works if you eventually earn an exclusive placement — don't pitch it as a swap.

## Tool options

Rewardful (Stripe-native), Tolt (SaaS-focused), PartnerStack (built for larger partner ecosystems — affiliates, resellers, agencies — rather than a simple affiliate list; changed hands in April 2026 — AppDirect acquired it to build what AppDirect calls a "unified subscription commerce platform for partner-led growth." Confirm at purchase time whether it's still sold as a standalone tool or now bundled with AppDirect's marketplace infrastructure, rather than assuming a pre-acquisition comparison article still describes current pricing and packaging), Introw (CRM-native B2B partner portal with deal registration, built on top of HubSpot/Salesforce rather than replacing them).
