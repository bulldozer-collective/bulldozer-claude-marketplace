# Layer 5: Creative and Copy Checks

- How many distinct creative variants ran in the last 30 days? Fewer than 5 = not testing. More than 30 = noise, no discipline
- Are variants tied to specific hypotheses (audience angle, format, message)? Random variants = random learnings
- Meta: since Andromeda (Meta's ad retrieval system, live since late 2024), visually or textually similar creatives get clustered under one Entity ID and only that one concept gets a shot at auction — 10 near-identical variants can collapse into a single entry. The target is genuinely distinct Entity IDs, not raw variant count: aim for creative that reads as a different concept, not a different crop of the same one. Fewer than 10 distinct concepts across a campaign's personas is thin
- Creative fatigue signals: frequency above 3 on prospecting = ad fatigue — pull it per campaign with `bdzGetAdAnalytics`. CPM rising while CTR falls = fatigue before frequency shows it. On LinkedIn, a native frequency cap (Campaign Manager, rolled out from July 2025) can now cap delivery at 3–30 impressions/member over a rolling 7-day window directly in campaign settings — a real fix instead of only creative refresh or manual budget throttling after the fact. It launched for brand awareness objectives specifically; verify it's actually offered before recommending it on a lead-gen or website-visit campaign
- Hooks: are the first 3 seconds of video tested separately from the body? Hook = 80% of video performance — `bdzGetAdAnalytics` (target `AD_ANALYTICS_TARGET_LAYER_3_ALL`) returns hookRate and videoAvgTimeWatchedSeconds per ad, and `bdzGetAdLayer3Creatives` returns the creative actually served (media, full copy, CTAs) to match against it
- Ad copy: is the headline benefit-first or feature-first? Benefit copy consistently outperforms feature copy for non-technical buyers
- **Competitor creative — pull it, don't guess it (Google only).** Before crediting or blaming this account's own creative for a performance shift, check what a named competitor is actually running: resolve their `companyId` (`bdzCreateCompetitor` if it doesn't exist yet), find their Google Ads Transparency Center advertiser ID with `bdzGetSeaPublicGoogleAdvertiserIds` (by domain) or `bdzSearchSeaPublicAdvertisers` (by keyword — the only one that answers "which advertisers run on keyword X"; it spends jetons per call, don't loop it), then `bdzCreateSeaPublicGoogleAdwords` — asynchronous, poll `bdzGetSeaPublicGoogleAdwordsImport` until SUCCESS or ERROR, nothing is readable before that — and read the imported creative with `bdzListSeaPublicGoogleAdwords`. A `null` ad-copy field means the Transparency Center never published readable text for that creative, not that it carries no message. This scraped public corpus never reconciles with the account's own private performance (`bdzListSeaL1s`/`L3s`) — it shows what a competitor says, not how it performs. Separately, `bdzCreateSeaPublicDataPage` pulls a Semrush paid-search snapshot for the same company by month/year (needs a website on file) for a keyword-level view of their spend pattern alongside the creative
