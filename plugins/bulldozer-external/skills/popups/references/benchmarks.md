# Popup Conversion Benchmarks

These are marketing-aggregator ranges (Omnisend, Popupsmart, Crazyegg — not audited academic studies), useful as a sanity check on a new popup, not as a target. Measure the real one — see *Measuring in Bulldozer OS* below.

| Popup type | Typical conversion rate |
|-----------|------------------------|
| Email popup (general) | 2–5% (2025 aggregates cluster near the low end, ~2–3.5%) |
| Exit intent | 3–10% (aggregates cluster ~4–7%; 10%+ needs a strong offer, e.g. a discount) |
| Click-triggered (self-selected) | 10%+ — and often much higher: this is the widest-varying category because clicking to open it is itself a strong intent signal; one aggregate (Popupsmart) reports ~50%+ for this type |
| Lead magnet (relevant) | 5–15% (B2B aggregates skew toward the 8–12% band) |

**These ranges themselves move between aggregators, not just between years** — a separate pass over 2026 sources put the *average* exit-intent popup closer to 2.8%, with only the top 10% reaching the ~19% mark this table's upper bound implies is typical. That swing between two sets of unaudited marketing blogs, in the same year, is itself the finding: don't anchor on the exact numbers above, confirm against the popup's own live numbers (below) before deciding whether it's underperforming.
