# Key metrics — formulas, market ranges, and sourcing caveats

Detail for the Key Metrics section of `churn-prevention`. Retention, save-rate, and
recovery-rate benchmarks don't transport across business models — a €12/mo self-serve tool
and a €2K/mo B2B seat contract don't churn the same way, and neither do their save flows.
Treat the ranges below as a sanity check on a new program, not a target to hit: compute each
metric from the product's own baseline before and after the cancel flow / dunning stack
ships, and judge the change against that baseline.

| Metric | Formula | Range seen in the market (2025–2026) |
|--------|---------|--------|
| Monthly churn rate | Churned / Start-of-month customers | Sources disagree not just on the number but on the period it describes — some 2026 aggregators cite "~3.5% median B2B SaaS churn" as an annual figure (≈0.3%/mo blended, in line with enterprise running well under 0.5%/mo), others cite the same 3.5% as a monthly figure for the same segment, a 10x gap. Confirm which period a source means before quoting it. A check (2026-09-25) found several 2026 aggregators titling this figure explicitly "3.5% monthly," with a segment breakdown that matches the row below (enterprise <0.5%/mo, mid-market 0.5–1.5%/mo, SMB/prosumer 2–4%/mo) plus a spread inside each segment — top-quartile operators reported under 1.2%/mo, bottom-quartile above 6%/mo. That tilts toward the monthly reading being the more common one this year, but none of the pages could be opened directly (blocked from this environment) to confirm methodology, so treat it as a lean, not a resolved conflict. Monthly bars commonly cited: well under 0.5%/mo for enterprise, 0.5–1.5%/mo mid-market, 2–4%/mo SMB/prosumer — varies heavily by ACV and contract length |
| Net revenue churn | (Lost MRR – Expansion MRR) / Start MRR | Negative = net expansion. See `customer-health-expansion` for NRR benchmarks by ARR band |
| Logo retention vs. NRR gap | Accounts retained / Accounts start, compared to NRR | Not a target — a diagnostic. A wide gap (e.g. logo retention 80% next to NRR 105%+) means expansion revenue is masking a real retention problem, not solving it — worth checking before reporting NRR alone as a health metric, especially on usage- or seat-based pricing |
| Cancel flow save rate | Saved / Total cancel sessions | Static cancel page: 5–10%. Dynamic, reason-specific offers: 15–25%. Best-in-class dynamic flows: up to ~34% (Churnkey's own reported book-wide rate — a vendor figure, not an independent audit, reconfirmed against multiple 2026 sources) |
| Pause reactivation rate | Reactivated / Total paused | No reliable named SaaS benchmark exists — don't anchor on a single fraction. The two closest named figures found (2026-10-09) contradict each other and likely don't measure the same thing: Churnkey's "Pause Wall" page (vendor, no methodology) reports a resume rate of 43–89% by organization; Visionary Marketing's "SaaS Cohort Study 2026" (n=38 SaaS clients, 184,000 customer-months) reports a 6.4% reactivation rate, down from 11.2% in 2022, with no certainty the figure measures paused rather than cancelled accounts. RevenueCat's State of Subscription Apps 2026 has a reactivation figure too, but for consumer mobile apps, not B2B SaaS — don't borrow it either. Track the product's own paused cohort's return rate from day one instead of quoting any of these as a target |
| Dunning recovery rate | Recovered / Total failed payments | Median across SaaS ~48%. No automation: 20–31%. Retries + email: 45–55%. Automated retry logic + card updater: 70–85% |
