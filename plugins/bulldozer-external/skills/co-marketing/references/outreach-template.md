# Partner outreach template

Referenced from "Partner Outreach Template" in the main skill.

```
Subject: [Your Company] + [Their Company] — quick idea

Hey [Name],

I'm [Role] at [Your Company]. We [one-line description].

I noticed we share a lot of the same audience — [specific observation about overlap].

I have an idea for a [specific campaign type] that could work well for both of us: [one-sentence pitch].

Would you be open to a quick call this week?

[Your name]
```

**Personalization rule**: Reference something specific — a recent post, a shared customer, or an event you both attended. Generic outreach goes unread.

**Don't claim an overlap you haven't checked.** "I noticed we share a lot of the same audience"
is only worth writing if you've actually looked — a manual cross-reference of named customers,
a Crossbeam match, or genuine engagement seen on their tracked LinkedIn posts. A partner
who checks and finds you were guessing doesn't get to a second email.

**Sending it**: for a short list of hand-picked candidates, send this yourself — it's relationship-building, not a blast. If the outreach scales past a handful of prospects (e.g., testing a partner category), Lemlist is the cold-outreach tool wired into Bulldozer OS (email, LinkedIn, call, SMS, WhatsApp steps) and can carry this sequence instead of a manual send.
