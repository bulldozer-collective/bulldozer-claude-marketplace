# 30/60/90-Day Onboarding Plan Template

## Section 5 — 30/60/90-day plan

```
30 Days — Learn
Goal: Understand the product, the ICP, the sales process, and the tools
Milestones:
- Complete product demo certification (can demo independently by day 15)
- Shadow 5 discovery calls with senior AE / quota-carrying peer. **If this is the first hire in the function, there is no peer to shadow** — substitute the founder, the manager, or whoever has been running these calls informally, and say so in the plan rather than leaving the milestone unmet by default
- Complete CRM training — pipeline hygiene and stage definitions, checked by the manager at day 30, not assumed
- Run first outbound sequence to their assigned segment, unpolished is fine — the point is reps, not results
- No solo customer-facing call before day 15; weekly 1:1 with manager from week 1

30-day check: manager confirms tool fluency and pipeline hygiene hands-on, not by self-report. A rep who still can't navigate the CRM unaided at day 30 is a signal to address now, not a phase to wait out.

60 Days — Contribute
Goal: Run discovery and demo calls independently; build a working pipeline
Milestones:
- Run discovery calls solo, with the manager shadowing roughly 1 in 5 for calibration
- Build a qualified pipeline at the coverage ratio the team already uses for this quota — don't invent a new one for this hire. **First hire in the function: there is no existing ratio to inherit** — set a provisional coverage target explicitly in this plan (funnel math from the quota and expected close rate, not a guess) and revisit it once real pipeline data exists
- Progress at least one deal past the mid-funnel stage
- Multi-thread at least two live opportunities beyond the first contact

60-day check: manager and hire review pipeline coverage and deal quality together, not activity volume alone. A rep hitting call and email targets with no pipeline movement is an early-warning sign, not a ramp issue to let run.

90 Days — Own
Goal: Carry a full slice of quota and operate without a training wheel
Milestones:
- Full quota ownership begins — partial credit before day 90 is a ramp adjustment agreed in advance, not a discount on the role
- Close at least one deal independently, start to finish
- Deliver one piece of structured feedback on the sales process, tooling, or messaging — a new hire's outside view is most useful exactly once, before it fades
- Manager and hire complete a written 90-day retro: what's working, and what the role actually requires that the scorecard or JD didn't capture

90-day check: this is the go/no-go point on the hire, not a formality. If a gap flagged in Stage 2 or 3 of the interview (Section 3) is still open at day 90, that's a miss in the interview process to fix for the next hire — onboarding cannot coach away a competence gap the process should have caught.
```
