# Competitive Intelligence Output — Brief Template

```
## Competitive Brief: [Competitor Name]

### Why they win (from buyer interviews)
[What buyers say the competitor does better — in buyer language, not your internal framing]

### Why you win against them (from buyer interviews)
[What buyers say you do better — in buyer language]

### Their sales process strengths (from buyer interviews)
[How their sales team operates — what buyers found valuable about working with them]

### Their weaknesses (from buyer interviews)
[What buyers found frustrating, unclear, or weak — this feeds directly into battlecards]

### Decision criteria where they dominate
[What evaluation dimensions they consistently win on]

### Landmines (questions that surface their weaknesses)
[Discovery questions that expose their gaps without naming them]
```

**If Claap is connected, use it to cross-check, never to replace, the brief above.** Recorded sales calls tagged `CompetitorMentions` (or `Objections`) surface what came up organically in live deals — useful to confirm a pattern the buyer interviews already found, or to flag a competitor worth watching before it hits 3+ mentions in interviews. Add `hasExternalSpeaker: true` to the search filters alongside the tag: on this workspace it measurably narrows the result set versus the tag alone, which means a tag match doesn't by itself guarantee a confirmed external participant in that recording — without the filter, a hit can be a rep recapping the objection to a colleague after the call, not the buyer raising it live. That's the same "buyers, not reps" discipline the whole program runs on, applied to this one cross-check. When a matching recording is linked to a deal, the result carries that deal's own size, type, and stage alongside the transcript — enough to sort a competitor mention into the same segment (deal-size band, `dealtype`) the pattern matrix already runs on, instead of pooling it in with every mention regardless of segment. It cannot substitute for buyer testimony on "why they win": that's a rep's account of the room, exactly the source this program exists to get past.
