# Target Stack by ARR Stage — Benchmarks

**Early Stage (<€5M ARR) — 5–7 tools, €30–80K/year:**
- CRM: HubSpot Sales Hub Pro or Salesforce Starter
- Enrichment: Apollo.io (email + firmographic, single tool)
- Outbound sequencing: HubSpot Sequences (if already on HubSpot) or Instantly
- Meeting scheduling: HubSpot Meetings (native, no extra cost)
- Analytics: Native CRM dashboards (no BI tool needed)
- Call recording: Fathom (recording/transcription free; AI summaries capped at 5/month on the free plan as of 2026 — confirm current caps before recommending) or Gong (if team is ≥5 AEs and call coaching is strategic)

**Growth Stage (€5–20M ARR) — 8–12 tools, €100–250K/year:**
- CRM: HubSpot or Salesforce
- Enrichment: Apollo + Clay (waterfall enrichment for outbound)
- Outbound sequencing: Outreach or Salesloft (one, not both)
- LinkedIn: LinkedIn Sales Navigator (Team)
- Meeting scheduling: Chili Piper (for inbound routing) or HubSpot Meetings
- Conversation intelligence: Gong
- BI: Looker Studio connected to CRM (or native Salesforce Analytics)
- ABM: LinkedIn Sponsored (no additional ABM platform needed until €15M+ ARR)

**Scale Stage (€20M+ ARR) — 12–20 tools, €250–500K/year:**
Add: Forecasting (Clari Forecast — same company as Salesloft under a unified brand since September 2026; if Salesloft is already the sequencing tool, treat this as a module of the existing vendor relationship, not a separate vendor to negotiate against — but not a free module either. As of 2026, deals bundling Salesloft with Clari's forecasting stack under the combined "Predictive Revenue System" story carry pricing above what either product cost standalone before the merger; a multi-year contract signed before the merger generally keeps its original terms only through its own current term, not at the next renewal. Budget the bundle's real renewal cost before assuming the merger is cost-neutral), advanced BI (Tableau or Metabase), ABM platform (6sense or Demandbase), advanced enrichment (ZoomInfo for enterprise contacts), territory management, CPQ

**These benchmarks assume one GTM motion.** A team running two at once — self-serve alongside enterprise outbound, for instance — won't fit a single stage row: a tool that scores well against the outbound motion's cost-per-outcome looks wasteful once blended with a self-serve team that never touches it, and a self-serve tool judged against outbound benchmarks fails for the same reason in reverse. When more than one motion is live, run Steps 2 through 5 separately per motion, and size the Step 6 target stack as the sum of each motion's own target rather than a single blended lookup.
