## Naming a framework isn't the same as gating on it

MEDDPICC is real and widely taught — the eight-letter extension of MEDDIC, the
six-letter framework built at PTC in the 1990s by Dick Dunkel and Jack Napoli. Which
training firm formalized the extra two letters (Paper Process, Competition) isn't
settled: Force Management and Andy Whyte's MEDDICC.com both market themselves as the
source, and neither claim holds up against a primary citation — so credit the framework
by name, not by inventor. Either way, the name only controls anything if the CRM has a
field for each of the eight letters and reps actually fill them in before a deal is
called qualified. If those fields don't exist yet, pick BANT or a two-line house version
reps will complete, rather than naming a framework the team can't practice — a name with
no fields behind it is a slide, not a qualification bar.
