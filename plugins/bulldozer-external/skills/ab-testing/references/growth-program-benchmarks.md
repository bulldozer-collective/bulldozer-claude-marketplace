# Growth experimentation program — velocity benchmarks

**Velocity targets**:
- 4–8 experiments launched per month
- 20–30% win rate is healthy (higher may indicate overly safe hypotheses)
- 2–4 week average test duration
- 20+ hypotheses in backlog at all times

These are mid-program targets, not a floor to hit immediately: 2026 industry figures put the
median company at 2-3 tests/month, with mature, well-resourced programs running 15-30+ — a
young program landing 4-8/month with real backlog discipline is already ahead of the median,
not behind it. And the velocity/win-rate numbers are health signals, not the goal: a slower
program that learns something applicable from most of its tests beats a faster one that ships
noise to hit a monthly count — track what each test taught, not just whether it "won."
