# Piliers de contenu et recyclage — gabarits de planification

À lire au moment de construire un calendrier éditorial (répartition des piliers) ou de
transformer un contenu long existant (article, podcast, étude de cas, webinaire) en formats
sociaux — pas pour rédiger un post isolé, qui relève plutôt de
`references/gabarits-ecriture.md`.

## Content Pillar Framework

Build content around 3–5 pillars that align with your expertise and business:

| Pillar | % of content | Example topics |
|--------|:------------:|---------------|
| Industry insights | 30% | Trends, data, predictions, observations |
| Educational | 25% | Frameworks, how-tos, principles |
| Behind the scenes | 20% | Building journey, decisions, lessons |
| Social proof | 15% | Customer wins, use cases, results |
| Promotional | 10% | Product updates, offers, launches |

**When to drop a pillar**: a pillar that hasn't produced a post in a full quarter comes out of the rotation — it doesn't get compensated for with more volume elsewhere. A five-pillar plan nobody is actually filling is a wish list, not a plan.

## Content Repurposing

Convert long-form content to social:

| Source | LinkedIn | Twitter/X | Instagram |
|--------|----------|-----------|-----------|
| Blog post | Extract 3 insights as text post | Thread with key points | Carousel with tips |
| Podcast episode | Quote or key lesson | Thread summary | Stories with quote |
| Customer case study | Story post with result | Tweet with metric | Before/after carousel |
| Webinar | 3 key takeaways post | Thread with frameworks | Slide carousel |

**Instagram note**: carousels and Reels are not interchangeable — Reels reach more new accounts (discovery), carousels earn more saves and total engagement from an audience that already follows the account (education, benchmarks, step-by-step). The direction holds across several named 2026 studies, but the size of the gap doesn't: Buffer (52M+ posts) measures carousels at 6.90% engagement by reach against 3.31% for Reels, with Reels still getting 36% more reach; Socialinsider separately measures Reels reach at roughly twice a carousel's. Treat the direction as solid, not the exact multiplier. For B2B, default to carousels for authority content and use Reels when the goal is reaching people who don't follow the account yet.

**Repurposing isn't reformatting.** If the LinkedIn or Twitter version could have been written from the source's headline alone — no number, no quote, no detail that proves someone actually read it — it's filler wearing the shape of repurposed content. Pull the one detail that wouldn't exist without the source, and build the shorter version around that.
