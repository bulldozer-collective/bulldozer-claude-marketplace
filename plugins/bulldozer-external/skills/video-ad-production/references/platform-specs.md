# Platform Specs to Check Before Rendering

**Meta** (third-party spec aggregators — Meta's own spec page wasn't reachable during this review, reverify before finalizing a production run): Feed 4:5 (1080×1350) or 1:1; Reels/Stories 9:16 (1080×1920). Practical runtime 15–60 sec on Feed and up to about 90 sec on Reels, well under the platform's much longer technical cap.

**LinkedIn** (official, linkedin.com/help/lms/answer/85306): 3 seconds to 30 minutes, with 15–30 seconds recommended so the ad qualifies for every placement. Aspect ratio 16:9, 1:1, 4:5 (the recommended vertical ratio), or 9:16 — each with its own minimum/maximum pixel range; check the exact range for the chosen ratio before export, and verify the current file-size ceiling in Campaign Manager's own upload flow (sources disagree on the exact number).
