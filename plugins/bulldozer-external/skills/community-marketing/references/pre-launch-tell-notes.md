# Pre-Launch Tell — why this is the single most common failure mode

This is the single most common reason branded communities fail: they're built as a content
distribution channel for the company (product updates, announcements, a place to push blog
posts) rather than a room the company earns the right to build because members were already
finding each other. A community with zero informal precedent is a bet, not a plan; size the
effort like one — a newsletter reply-all thread, a recurring AMA, or an existing forum is the
right-sized first move when nobody is already doing this unprompted, not a dedicated platform
and a four-week launch plan.
