# Launch Playbook — why Week 4 is earned, not scheduled

**Don't move to Week 4 on the calendar alone.** The whole point of Weeks 1–3 is to arrive at
Week 4 with a room that already has a pulse — the gate to open publicly isn't "three weeks have
passed," it's "founding members post without being prompted." A big public launch onto a quiet
room is worse than a slow one: most of the strangers who show up in the first 48 hours of a
public announcement never come back once, and the impression that sticks — "I checked it out,
nobody was talking" — is much harder to undo than a delayed launch date. If by the end of Week
3 staff is still writing the majority of unprompted posts (not replies — first posts), hold
Week 4 and repeat Week 2's culture-building instead of opening on schedule. A loud launch onto
an empty room isn't a shortcut, it's spending your one shot at a first impression on a room
that isn't ready for guests.
