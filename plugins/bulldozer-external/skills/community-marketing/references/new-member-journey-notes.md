# New Member Journey — why the activation moment is a reply, not a join, and the welcome DM copy

## Why "joined" isn't the activation moment

For a community, the activation moment isn't "joined" — joining costs a click and predicts
nothing. The moment that actually predicts whether someone sticks around is *a member's first
post gets a reply from someone other than staff, within the same day.* That single event is what
the three steps in the main skill file exist to engineer: Step 1 gets someone to post, Step 2
exists because most people won't on their own, and the entire point of "reply to every post" in
Week 1–2 of the launch playbook is to guarantee the reply half of this moment happens while the
room is small enough that staff can cover it. If a new member posts and gets silence, the
automated journey did its job and the community still failed them — track "posted but got no
reply within 24h" as its own count, separate from "never posted at all." They're different
failures with different fixes: one is an invitation problem, the other is a room-is-too-quiet
problem.

```
Hey [Name]! Welcome to the [Community Name] community.

1. Introduce yourself in #introductions
2. Get help or share a tip in #help-and-support
3. Check out #resources for the best community content

Happy to have you here.
— [Community Manager Name]
```
