# Search Console Data Pitfalls

- **A trap that appears fixed — keep the habit anyway.** The `dimension` parameter was unreliable server-side twice in 2026 (Aug 31, then Sep 2): a call made with `dimension: PAGE` returned QUERY rows mixed into the PAGE rows of the same response. A direct call on Sep 19 came back clean on both sides — a `dimension: PAGE` request returned 10/10 PAGE rows, a `dimension: QUERY` request returned 15/15 QUERY rows, no cross-contamination either way. Treat the mixing as resolved rather than as a live bug, but keep checking each returned row's own `dimension` field before you aggregate anything — the check costs nothing to keep, and one clean day doesn't retire it for good. Never sum PAGE rows with QUERY/COUNTRY/DEVICE rows regardless of the fix: they measure different things and aren't additive.
- **The normal freshness regime, and when to suspect an incident instead.** Search Console's own data lags the live site by 2–3 days (Google's documented publication delay, not an OS quirk) — pull a window that ends a few days before today, not today itself, or the most recent days will read as a traffic drop that's actually just data still catching up. That regime has broken at least three times in under a year: the Performance report ran weeks behind in December 2025 (resolved), the Page Indexing report ran about a month behind from mid-June to early July 2026 (resolved), and a third Performance report delay ran from roughly 18 to 26 September 2026 (confirmed by Google on the 21st, and reported back to normal shortly after) — a direct check on 30 September 2026 showed the freshest stored row sitting exactly at D-3. A brief wobble followed that clean reading — two checks in early October read D-4 then D-5 instead of the expected D-3 — but it reversed on its own: a direct recheck on 7 October (same project) landed back exactly on D-3, and no SEO press covered an October 2026 delay. Read that wobble as the kind of short-lived variance any daily scheduler produces, not an emerging fourth incident. A further independent recheck on 11 October confirmed the same D-3 regime still holding, four days of stability since the 7 October reading — treat the regime as settled rather than re-litigate it on every audit, while keeping the underlying habit. The operating habit stays the same regardless: if the most recent days of a client's data look stuck well past D-3 for several days running, check for a live incident before either concluding a real traffic drop or blaming the OS — a couple of isolated D-4/D-5 reads is not that signal on its own, a sustained multi-day run is.
- **A fourth trap, this one about history rather than lag.** A Google-side Search Console logging bug inflated impressions (and, by construction, average position and CTR) from 13 May 2025 to 27 April 2026 — clicks were not affected. Any comparison this audit makes against a baseline inside that window — a "traffic 6 months ago" reference, a pre-update number from the Rules section below — should be read on clicks alone; treat the impressions/position/CTR delta for that period as unreliable rather than as a real signal either way.
- **A newer Search Console report this stack can't reach yet.** Google introduced a separate "Search Generative AI performance" report, announced 3 June 2026 (testing started on a UK subset) and generalized mid-to-late August 2026 — press accounts split between an 11 August expansion "to every property" (later qualified by Google's own John Mueller as not yet every domain) and a 31 August date for full global availability; treat those as two different milestones of the same rollout rather than competing answers, and don't lean on either as the exact day. The report shows impressions of the site's pages inside Google AI Overviews, AI Mode and AI-powered Discover, broken down by page/country/device/date (no clicks, no CTR, no query). It is not exposed by the Search Analytics API that `bdzGetGscDomainAnalytics`/`bdzGetGscDomainAnalyticsSummary` wrap — that API's `type` parameter still only accepts web/image/video/news/discover/googleNews, nothing generative; the only way to pull this data today is the manual CSV export button on the report itself inside the Search Console UI. This is free, official data, but only reachable by opening Search Console directly, not through Bulldozer OS — don't let a library-wide organic-performance pull in this audit imply it's already covered.
